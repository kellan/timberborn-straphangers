STRAPHANGERS DEVELOPMENT REPLAY

Development builds currently record automatically. Load a colony, let several
trips occur, then return to the main menu or quit normally. The capture seals
on unload/quit or when its bounded size cap is reached. Normal station counting
continues after a recording fills. A force quit may leave an incomplete capture.

Each recording folder contains its own frozen auditor and station reference.
After the capture seals:
  python3 audit_recording.py pack <recording-folder> <bundle.zip>
  python3 audit_recording.py audit <recording-folder-or-zip> --output report.json

The auditor uses Python's standard library, with no game or .NET dependency.
It checks checksums, ordered records, authored doorway geometry, completed
native movement, counter events, daily arithmetic and tube membership deltas.
Native actor capture is bounded to 64 concurrent selected passengers. Larger
colonies are partial coverage. Displayed tube occupancy may lag or skip pieces;
missing occupancy is unconfirmed use, not proof that no ride occurred.

A -> A excursions are retained across tube transfers and construction gaps.
Already present tube occupants are baselines, never fabricated new entries.
Several trips within one rendered frame cannot be precisely disambiguated.

Replay end totals cover only the capture window and selected actors. The game
panel's saved lifetime totals cover a different scope. Do not compare them as
though they should be equal.

Only three recognized recording sessions are retained, with limits of 16 MiB
compressed / 128 MiB raw per trace, plus a final complete record and metadata.
Exported bundles are not automatically pruned. Review entity IDs and route data
before sharing a recording publicly. Diagnostic-off release mode is planned.
